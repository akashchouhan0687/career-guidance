Common Documents Checklist

Most government and institutional scholarship portals require digital scans of these standard documents. Keep clear, verified copies ready before applying:

Academic Marksheets

Class 10, Class 12, or last passed examination marksheet / transcript.

Identity Proof

Aadhaar Card, Student ID, or government-issued photo identity proof.

Income Certificate

Current financial year certificate issued by an authorized revenue officer (Tehsildar / SDO).

Caste / Category Certificate

Competent authority certificate for SC, ST, OBC, or Minority declaration, if applicable.

Domicile Certificate

Proof of permanent state residence, especially for state welfare schemes (e.g., MahaDBT).

Bank Account Details

Active bank savings passbook copy in the student's name, seeded with Aadhaar for DBT transfer.

Admission / Bonafide Certificate

College admission allotment letter, fee payment receipt, or bonafide student letter.

Disability Certificate (If Applicable)

Valid certificate issued by an authorized government medical board with ≥40% disability.

Important Notice: Document requirements vary across Central, State, and Institutional schemes. Students must always read the latest official scheme guidelines before submitting applications on the portal.

Helpful Application Guidance

Follow these structured steps to ensure your scholarship application is processed smoothly:

1

Check Eligibility Carefully

Review the minimum qualifying percentage, income limits, eligible course branches, and category criteria before initiating your application.

2

Verify the Current Deadline

Official timelines and portal windows for fresh and renewal applications are updated every academic year. Always verify active closing dates.

3

Prepare Required Documents

Scan your original certificates clearly within the permissible file format and file size limits specified by the portal.

4

Apply Through the Official Portal

Register via One-Time Registration (OTR) on the National Scholarship Portal (scholarships.gov.in) or your state portal (e.g. MahaDBT).

5

Keep Application Reference

Save and print the final submitted application form, acknowledgement receipt, and Application ID for future reference and institute scrutiny.

6

Track Application on Portal

Log in regularly to check institute verification, nodal officer remarks, or defect rectification notices to prevent disqualification.
